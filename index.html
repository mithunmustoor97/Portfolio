Product Thinking

How decisions get made

Beyond screens - friction to systems to measurable outcomes.

01 - Observe

Quiet observation

Watch real users in context. Surface friction others miss - paper workflows, low literacy, enterprise scan patterns.

02 - Frame

Problem before pixels

Name the villain: chaos, missing pages, WCAG risk.

03 - System

Clarity over decoration

Modular systems, linear flows, progressive disclosure.

04 - Prove

Outcomes, not outputs

Faster onboarding, reduced drop-off, sales enablement, WCAG compliance - design that earns trust in the field and the boardroom.

// About Me

Quiet observation is the product edge.

As an introverted designer, I find my greatest asset is the power of quiet observation. I watch user interactions closely, identifying friction points that often go unnoticed, translating user behaviors into highly deliberate, peaceful digital workflows.

Away from layout structures and system wireframes, I enjoy the quiet rhythm of small things - brewing a morning pour-over, walking in calm nature, or sharing deeply meaningful times with my closest circle. I strive to cultivate clean layouts that let digital products breathe.
